==============================================================================
CONCLUSION
==============================================================================
The hypothetical EV of acting was POSITIVE (INR 18,711.78), computed against an estimator deliberately primed to be optimistic (85% success rate assumed).
The economist's actual verdict was 'veto' with plan () - every proposed action was removed by C1 before the EV arithmetic that produced that positive number was ever allowed to decide anything.
No expected value, however large, overrode the risk-decline veto. This is the property tests/test_economist.py::test_c1_vetoes_a_risk_decline_at_any_expected_value already asserts with an amount two orders of magnitude larger; this trace shows the same guarantee at a realistic transaction size, in full.
